FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the System, on-device AI privacy, and Hunter progression.

Is the SYSTEM app completely free to use?
Yes! The core Hunter experience — including the Daily Quest engine, TensorFlow AI camera rep tracking, and Hunter Rank progression — is 100% free forever. An optional Monarch Guild Pass unlocks premium voice packs, customized holographic HUD themes, and advanced AFK Shadow Mining multipliers.
How does the AI camera rep tracking work, and is my camera feed private?
Your privacy is guarded with absolute zero-compromise encryption. We use lightweight TensorFlow.js models that execute 100% locally on your device’s GPU/NPU. Your camera feed is processed in volatile memory to extract skeletal coordinates and immediately discarded. No video, photo, or audio streams ever leave your phone.
Do I need gym equipment or a fitness membership?
None required! Just like Sung Jin-Woo's humble beginnings, the foundational workouts focus on calisthenics and bodyweight mastery: push-ups, squats, sit-ups, pull-ups, and running. For advanced hunters with gym access, the System allows custom quest modifiers (bench press, deadlifts, and kettlebell swings).
What actually happens if I fail the Daily Quest before midnight?
If the midnight timer reaches 00:00:00 without completing your daily quota, the System issues a critical warning. To lift the penalty and regain full stat gains, you must clear a timed cardio survival quest (running or brisk walking) to "evade" the giant centipedes of the Penalty Zone desert.
How does GPS Dungeon Hunting work in real cities?
The System overlays dynamic Gate coordinates onto real-world streets, parks, and landmarks using geolocation. As you walk or jog, your movement clears the "Fog of War" on your radar. Approaching within 50 meters of a Gate triggers the dungeon raid encounter where reps deal combat damage to the guardian boss.
Can I synchronize my data with Apple Health, Google Fit, and Garmin?
Yes, full bidirectional sync is supported for Apple HealthKit, Google Health Connect, and Garmin Connect. Your daily step count, resting heart rate, running distance, and sleep analysis automatically feed into your Hunter HP/MP restoration stats.
