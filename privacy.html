SECURITY & PRIVACY POLICY

In the world of Hunters, intelligence is life. We guard your physical telemetry, camera vision, and biometric stats with zero-compromise encryption.

PROTOCOL CODE: #SYS-SEC-2026-V2 LAST COMPILED: SEPTEMBER 2026 STATUS: 100% ON-DEVICE ACTIVE

1. 100% On-Device AI Camera Vision (Shadow Scanner Mode)

The cornerstone of SYSTEM: Awakening is absolute visual privacy. When utilizing our AI Camera Rep Tracking:

  • Local Neural Execution: All computer vision models (TensorFlow.js) execute strictly on your device's local GPU or Neural Engine via WebGL/WebGPU shaders.
  • Zero Frame Storage: Camera image frames exist solely in volatile hardware RAM for less than 16 milliseconds per frame to calculate 17 skeletal joint coordinates (elbows, shoulders, hips, knees).
  • No Cloud Uplink: Image feeds, snapshots, face scans, or video files are never recorded, cached on disk, or uploaded to external servers.
  • Holographic Silhouette: What you see on screen is an abstract neon wireframe drawn over a black canvas — your private physical space remains completely invisible to the network.

2. Geolocation & Real-World GPS Gate Obfuscation

Our real-world GPS Gates and Fog of War map turn physical outdoor walking and running into dungeon raids. To protect your real-life security:

  • Geospatial Hashing: Coordinates are obfuscated using localized spatial hashing algorithms. We do not track or log specific residential addresses or private habitations.
  • Public Gate Placement: Magical Gates spawn strictly in designated public zones (parks, pedestrian squares, hiking trails, city monuments) and never on private property or restricted zones.
  • Private Route Telemetry: Your running paths and step routes remain encrypted on your device and are never shared publicly on competitive leaderboards without your explicit authorization.

3. Biometric & Health Data Synchronization

When you connect Apple HealthKit, Google Health Connect, or Garmin:

  • We request read-only access strictly for active calories burned, daily step counts, running distance, and resting heart rate.
  • This data is solely used to calculate your in-app Hunter HP/MP recovery rates and muscular fatigue heatmaps.
  • Health metrics are never correlated with advertising IDs or shared with health insurance providers, data brokers, or commercial syndicates.

4. Data Storage & Quantum-Grade Encryption

All account credentials, Hunter ID cards, inventory gear, and Shadow Army rosters are protected using military-grade encryption:

[SYSTEM ENCRYPTION STANDARD]: AES-256-GCM local storage encryption with TLS 1.3 transport security for all Guild communications.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to any commercial advertisers. You are the Hunter; your data is your mana.

5. User Rights & Nuclear Data Wipe

Under international regulations (GDPR, CCPA, and Korean Personal Information Protection Act), you maintain total autonomy over your data:

  • Data Export: Download an unencrypted copy of your full Hunter quest logs, workout records, and rank history in standard JSON format at any time.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Triggering the in-app "System Resignation / Wipe" permanently purges all cloud backups, local cache, and guild memberships within 24 hours.
