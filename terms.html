3. Real-World GPS Exploration Safety Guidelines

When exploring real-world GPS Gates or clearing Fog of War across your neighborhood:

  • Situational Awareness: NEVER stare at your phone screen while crossing streets, navigating vehicular traffic, or operating bicycles, skateboards, or motor vehicles. Always stop in a safe, secure pedestrian area before checking Gate coordinates.
  • Respect Private Property: NEVER trespass on private residences, commercial restricted property, construction sites, rail lines, or hazardous terrain to reach a Gate. If a Gate is inaccessible, use the in-app "Relocate Gate" command.
